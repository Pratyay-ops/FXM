FXM 0.1.0 is the first release of FXM, a free and open-source music player. It runs in your browser: open the web app at https://fxmusic.pages.dev/app/, or install it from the browser so that it opens in its own window. There is nothing to sign up for and no FXM server: your library, history and settings stay on your device unless you turn on sync, and the songs you play go to Last.fm or ListenBrainz only if you connect an account for scrobbling.

FXM is in early development: what isn't released yet is listed under "Coming soon" below.

## Get it

- **The web app** at https://fxmusic.pages.dev/app/, which most browsers can install as an app.
- **FXM Connect**, the small browser extension that lets the web app reach YouTube Music and Spotify from your own browser, on a computer. For Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers, download `fxm-connect-chromium-0.1.0.zip` below and load it unpacked, as [its install steps](https://github.com/Pratyay-ops/FXM/blob/v0.1.0/apps/extension/README.md#install) describe. Keep Developer mode on, because Chrome turns unpacked extensions off without it. FXM Connect doesn't update itself: to update it, unzip each new release over the same folder and reload it on the browser's extensions page. It comes to Firefox Add-ons and Edge Add-ons once it is published there. Until then, Firefox can load `fxm-connect-firefox-0.1.0.zip` as a temporary add-on, which Firefox removes when it restarts.

## What plays

- **YouTube Music**, with FXM Connect: practically any song, in two modes. Music plays songs only, from any source; Music + Video also plays any YouTube video, such as covers, live sets and remixes, as music. Without FXM Connect, YouTube Music plays in the experimental Lite tier where the browser allows it. Lite relies on a way of reaching YouTube Music that YouTube doesn't document, so it may stop working at any time.
- **Internet Archive**, everywhere, with or without FXM Connect: free music, including lossless FLAC.
- **Your own music files**, from a folder or with a file picker, with their tags and covers.

On phones and tablets, and in browsers such as Safari, FXM Connect can't be added yet, so the web app plays Internet Archive and your own files there, and YouTube Music in the Lite tier if the browser allows it: Lite isn't tested in Safari or on phones yet. The Android app, which won't need FXM Connect, is coming soon.

## Also in this release

- **Spotify, built in and switchable.** With FXM Connect, search Spotify's catalog and see related artists. Switch Spotify off in Settings and FXM doesn't talk to it at all.
- **Your library.** Likes, playlists in an order you choose, your listening history, and backups you can export and import.
- **Imports.** Bring in your playlists, liked songs and listening history from Spotify's data download, a CSV file (such as one Exportify saves) or an M3U playlist, or paste a link to a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist, or, with FXM Connect, a Spotify playlist, album or song. FXM finds each song on YouTube Music, with FXM Connect or in the Lite tier, and lists the ones it isn't sure of for you to check.
- **Sync between your devices**, off until you turn it on in Settings: your library, your settings and the plays of the last 90 days, kept in a secret gist on your own GitHub account with a token you create. The [privacy page](https://fxmusic.pages.dev/privacy) says who can read the gist.
- **A home feed made for you**, wherever YouTube Music plays, with FXM Connect or in the Lite tier: quick picks, daily mixes, Discover and more, put together on your device from YouTube Music radio and ListenBrainz, and with FXM Connect also from Spotify while it is on and Last.fm's stations. The first visit asks which artists you like. Without YouTube Music, Home shows what you play.
- **Downloads** for offline listening, as the file the source has: Internet Archive's in any browser, YouTube Music's with FXM Connect. They play first, also without a connection.
- **Audio quality** in Settings: one streaming quality for Wi-Fi and one for mobile data, and a data saver. YouTube audio is Opus or AAC and never lossless; lossless FLAC comes from sources that have it, such as Internet Archive.
- **Synced lyrics** from LRCLIB, which follow the song line by line.
- **Scrobbling** to Last.fm and ListenBrainz, off until you connect an account in Settings.
- **SponsorBlock**, off until you turn it on in Settings: in Music + Video, it skips the intros, skits and outros that SponsorBlock's community marks in music videos, without telling SponsorBlock which video plays. Songs always play in full.
- **Matches are suggestions.** When a song comes from another source, such as a Spotify track or an imported playlist, FXM finds its recording on YouTube Music and shows how sure the match is. A song you tap plays FXM's best guess, and "Not the right version?" lets you choose another. In a queue, songs with a best guess are skipped until you review them, unless you turn on "Play best guesses". Playing sure matches automatically isn't available yet: it comes once FXM's matching has been tested on enough songs.
