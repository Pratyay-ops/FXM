FXM 0.2.1 fixes YouTube downloads and the equalizer for people whose connection goes through a company VPN or a security proxy, makes downloads say why when they can't finish, and changes Download so it saves a file in the format you choose.

## Get it

- **The web app** at https://fxmusic.pages.dev/app/ updates itself: if you have it open, it finds the new version within about an hour and says "A new version of FXM is ready". Choose Reload, or it starts the next time you open FXM.
- **Update FXM Connect to 0.2.1.** The fix for downloads and the equalizer behind a VPN or proxy is in the extension, so this update matters. FXM Connect doesn't update itself: download `fxm-connect-chromium-0.2.1.zip` below, unzip it over the same folder you loaded FXM Connect from, then press Reload on its card on the browser's extensions page (`chrome://extensions`, `edge://extensions` or `brave://extensions`). In Firefox, load `fxm-connect-firefox-0.2.1.zip` as a temporary add-on. Until you update, FXM tells you when an older FXM Connect is the reason something can't work.

## Fixed

- **YouTube downloads and the equalizer behind a VPN or proxy** ([#10](https://github.com/Pratyay-ops/FXM/issues/10)). On some networks, YouTube sends a song's audio from a different server than the one it first named. FXM Connect 0.2.0 couldn't read the audio after that move, so downloads never finished and the equalizer couldn't process YouTube songs, although the songs played. FXM Connect 0.2.1 keeps the audio readable after the move. It still does so only for requests FXM itself makes.
- **Downloads no longer wait forever** ([#11](https://github.com/Pratyay-ops/FXM/issues/11)). When a song's audio can't be read, its download now stops within seconds and its row says why and what to do, instead of staying on "Downloading".
- **The Queue button closes the queue** ([#12](https://github.com/Pratyay-ops/FXM/issues/12)). Press it again to go back to the page you opened the queue from.
- **The equalizer says why it can't process a song**: an older FXM Connect to update, a browser that didn't let FXM Connect read the audio, or a first tap or click that starts it.

## Changed

- **Download saves a file** ([#13](https://github.com/Pratyay-ops/FXM/issues/13)). Choosing Download now asks which format you want (the original file, or FLAC, ALAC, WAV, MP3, AAC or Opus, each saying what you get) and saves the file to your device, and FXM still keeps its offline copy in your Downloads playlist. In Chrome and Edge, the original file's type is only known once it has downloaded, so FXM downloads it first and then asks where to save it; every other format asks first. An album or a playlist asks once, and in Chrome and Edge you choose one folder for all its songs. To keep only the offline copy, as before, switch off "Save a file to this device when downloading" in Settings → Downloads.

## Also in this release

- **A "YouTube audio" check in Settings → Diagnostics** says whether FXM Connect can read YouTube's audio in this browser, which YouTube downloads and the equalizer need, and why not when it can't. **Copy report** copies a summary of your diagnostics to share when something doesn't work. It leaves out addresses, links, tokens and cookies.
