FXM Connect

Version Development build

FXM Connect lets the FXM web app reach music services (YouTube, Spotify and Last.fm) from your own browser. It adds the headers those services need to answer FXM and, only when you opt in below, reads your Spotify login or your YouTube Music library. It has no analytics or tracking and runs no remote code.

Development build. It treats any app served on the localhost origins listed below as FXM, including other projects and their dependencies. Keep both account access options off while using localhost, or use a separate browser profile. Never publish this build.

Spotify

Off by default. When on, the FXM app can ask FXM Connect for a Spotify access token for the account you are signed in to at open.spotify.com in this browser. The app asks for one only after you also connect your Spotify account in FXM. That account sync is experimental: it has been tested with a stand-in for Spotify, not yet with real accounts. FXM Connect fetches the token from open.spotify.com with your browser's existing Spotify session and passes FXM only the token response. It never reads, stores or shares your cookies. Spotify's terms don't permit automated access like FXM's, and they let Spotify suspend accounts that break them; no such case has been reported for this kind of use.

Firefox asks for the optional authentication-information consent before either account bridge can be used. FXM Connect keeps browser credentials inside the extension; no FXM server receives your cookies or account credentials. You can withdraw this consent in Firefox's add-on settings at any time.

Development build only, off by default, and only in effect while the setting above is on. Any app on the localhost origins below could then get a token for your Spotify account.

YouTube Music

Off by default. When enabled, FXM Connect uses the cookies already held by your browser for music.youtube.com to read your Liked Music, playlists, saved albums and followed artists. A complete saved-songs list is not currently exposed reliably. It asks for the optional browser cookies permission. Cookie values stay inside FXM Connect and are never sent to the FXM page or a server; only normalized music metadata is returned to the local app. The import is read-only and experimental: YouTube Music's internal interface is unofficial and can change. Turning this off revokes the optional cookies permission.
