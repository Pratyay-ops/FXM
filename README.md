# FXM

**FXM (Free Cross-platform Media)** is a free, open-source music player for the web, as an installable app, and on Android. It is in early development.

- **Play practically any song.** YouTube Music and YouTube are the catalog, with two explicit modes:
  - **Music** (catalog songs only, clean album audio)
  - **Music + Video** (also covers, live sets, remixes and any YouTube video)
- **Multiple sources and formats.** JioSaavn (AAC 320), Internet Archive and Jamendo (**real FLAC**), and your own files. Downloads can be saved as Original, FLAC, ALAC, WAV, Opus, AAC or MP3.
- **Made for you.** Spotify-style home feed, daily mixes and endless radio, computed on your device from YouTube Music radio, Last.fm and ListenBrainz.
- **Imports.** Spotify and YouTube playlists by URL, CSV and M3U.
- **No accounts, no ads from FXM, no tracking.**

Inspired by [Bloomee](https://github.com/HemantKArya/BloomeeTunes) and [Spotube](https://github.com/KRTirtho/spotube).
